02

Operational definitions

The variables and coding rules used to describe corner kicks and throw-ins.

Download the full definitions table · Word

Corners

Delivery type
Inswinger
Ball that goes through the air in a curve towards the goal.
Outswinger
Ball that goes through the air in a curve away from the goal
Straight
Ball that was kicked with no curve
Short(indirect)
The ball that was kicked to the nearest attacking player who is in the closest proximity to the initial corner kick.
(StatsBomb pass_height = ground pass; mean 14.5m (38-48m aerial) = indirect corner (Power et al., 2018)
Target zone
Near post
Delivery of ball that is targeted at nearest goal post of the penalty area.
Far post
Delivery of ball that is targeted at furthest goal post of the penalty
Central
Delivery of ball that is targeted at central area of penalty area
Player involvement
Number of attackers
Number of attackers that were present within the penalty area during the moment of set piece (Maneiro et al., 2021)

Range: attacker involvement was categorised as 0–5 or 6+ (Lee & Mills, 2021)
Number of defenders
Number of defenders that were present within the penalty area during the moment of set piece.

Range: defender involvement was categorised as 0–7 or 8+
Marking structure
Zonal
Defenders occupy fixed reference zones of the goal area, holding position regardless of the specific attacking movement (FIFA training centre, 2025)
Man-marking
Each player is individually assigned to track a specific attacker (Casal et al., 2015; Maneiro et al., 2021)
Hybrid
A defined mix of zonal and man-marking assignment within the same defensive set up (Casal et al., 2015; Maneiro et al., 2021)
Contact
First contact
Successful: A player from attacking team makes first contact with the ball post-delivery without any interference from the opposition

Unsuccessful: A player from defending team wins the first contact with the ball from following corner kick.
Second contact
Successful: A player from the attacking team makes the subsequent touch following first contact without any defensive interruption.
Unsuccessful: a player from the defending team makes the touch immediately following first contact
Retention
Possession retention
Successful: attacking team maintain control of the ball beyond first and second contact until the set piece phase is concluded

Unsuccessful: Defending team gains control of the ball via a turnover ending the attacking sequence
Shooting area
Near post
Shot taken at left side of the goal area
Far post
Shot taken at furthest post of the goal area
Central
Shot taken at the central side of the penalty area
Shooting
Shot created
Shot taken from corners
Shot on target from corners
Deliberate attempt to score that is on target from corner delivery.
Goals scored from corner
Count of goals scored from corner situations

Throw-ins

Ball contact
First contact
Successful: A player from the same team which throws the ball into play makes first contact with the ball post throw-in without an opposition player making contact.

Unsuccessful: A player from the opposition team which throws the ball into play makes first contact with the ball post throw-in.
Retention
Possession retention
Successful: The ball remains in the possession of the throwing team for 7 second or longer from the point of throw (Stone et al., 2021)

Unsuccessful: The defending team gains control of the ball within the 7 seconds of the throw (Stone et al., 2021)
Direction
Forward
The ball is thrown at 0-60 relative to the sideline towards the offensive goal
Lateral
The ball is thrown at 60-120 relative to the sideline
Backward
The ball is thrown at 120-180 relative to the sideline towards the halfway line
Length
Short
The ball was thrown a distance between 0-10 yards (0-9 meters)
Medium
The ball was thrown a distance between 10-20 yards (9.1-18.2m)
Long
The ball was thrown 20 yards or longer (>18.2m)
Shooting
Shot created
Shot event occurs when the player attempted a shot at goal from a throw in
Shot outcome
Shot outcome
It defines the result of a scoring attempt (StatsBomb, 2021)

Source: Casal et al., (2016); Lee & Mills (2021); Maneiro et al., (2021); StatsBomb (2021, 2022); Stone et al., (2021); FIFA Training Centre, (2025)
